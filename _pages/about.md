I am an M.S. student in Computer Science at UCLA, where I also received my B.S. in Computer Science.
I work on **machine learning systems**. My research interests include efficient serving of large language models and video generation / world models, KV-cache and state management, and memory-efficient generative systems.

Currently, I lead **BranchFlow** (UCLA & DiDi Global), a serving abstraction for interactive video generation that supports arbitrary-time rollback, branching, and exact resumption without retaining the full historical KV cache.
I also lead the serving-side research of **AdaptiveRL** at DiDi Global, which asks when the computation saved by adaptive layer skipping turns into end-to-end LLM serving gains.

I contribute to open-source ML infrastructure, including [VeRL](https://github.com/verl-project/verl) / [VeRL-Omni](https://github.com/verl-project/verl-omni) and [vLLM-Omni](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni).

Previously, I interned at DiDi Global, VMware, and Momenta. See my [CV](/cv/) for details.
